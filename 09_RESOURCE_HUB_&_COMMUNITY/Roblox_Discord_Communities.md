# Roblox Discord Communities

> **TL;DR:** Discord is where devs **show work daily**: VFX reels, combat prototypes, ODM/movement tech, UI shots. It's the best *inspiration* firehose, but it's Tier 5: unverified, often missing context, and sometimes misleading. This vault's workflow is **human-curated**. Copy a showcase link plus a short description into `_Inbox/`, and the agent breaks it down, verifies it against docs and writes `forum-archive` notes. Agents can't (and mustn't) scrape Discord: it needs a logged-in account, and automated scraping violates Discord's Terms.

## Servers referenced by this vault

## How to capture a showcase (for humans)
```markdown
<!-- _Inbox/2026-10-27_rain-puddles-showcase.md -->
source: https://discord.com/channels/<server>/<channel>/<message>
author: @handle (credit them)
what: puddle ripples + wet asphalt darkening synced with rain intensity, ~60 FPS on mobile claimed
media: (screenshot or clip description)
my question: how are ripples done? decals? EditableImage? particles?
```
The agent then applies the [[Showcase_Breakdown_Method]] and links results into notes like [[Wetness_Puddles_and_Drying]] or [[VFX_Composition_and_Timing]].

## Etiquette & safety
- Credit authors and respect "don't repost" requests. Store links, not their assets.
- Never paste DMs, personal info, or anything from private channels you were asked to keep private.
- Ignore and report anything offering exploits, "free Robux" or account trading ([[Exploit_Threat_Model]]).
- Hiring and commissions: use official channels (Roblox Talent Hub) and written agreements.
