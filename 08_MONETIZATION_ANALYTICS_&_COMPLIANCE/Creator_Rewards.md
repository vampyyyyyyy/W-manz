# Creator Rewards

> **TL;DR:** Creator Rewards (which replaced Engagement-Based Payouts and Creator Affiliates on **July 24, 2025**) pay Robux for two things. **Daily Engagement:** **5 Robux** per *Active Spender* who plays your game **10+ minutes** in a day, if it's one of the **first three** games they launch that day. **Audience Expansion:** a **35% revenue share on the first $100** of a New or Reactivated user's platform-wide qualifying purchases over **60 days**, when they arrive via your Share Link, direct link or a search for your game *and* your game keeps **100+ average DAU** for those 60 days.

## Definitions that matter
- **Active Spender:** active 60+ days and spent ≥ **$9.99** on qualifying purchases (Robux, Premium, UGC subscriptions) in the last 60 days.
- **Lapsed → Reactivated:** no login for 60 consecutive days, then returns (not via a Roblox campaign).
- Paid-access games **are** eligible.

## What this means for game design
1. **Be a "first three" game:** daily reasons to start a session with you (daily quests, streaks, timed events) ([[Retention_and_Engagement_Metrics]]).
2. **Earn the 10 minutes:** a first session that reaches fun fast, plus loops that naturally run past 10 minutes ([[Analytics_Dashboard_and_Metrics]]).
3. **Bring people from off-platform:** tracked Share Links in TikTok, YouTube and Discord posts, where showcase culture already lives ([[Roblox_Discord_Communities]]).
4. Track it all in Creator Hub ▸ Analytics (experience and share-link reward data).
