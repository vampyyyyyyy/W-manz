# Roblox Wallet Transition (RDC 2026)

> **TL;DR:** Official (DevForum "Introducing Roblox Wallet and Card: Get Paid Faster", Sept 2026): starting **December 2026**, **Roblox Wallet** rolls out to **U.S.-based independent creators (sole proprietors) aged 18+**, with **automatic USD payouts every business day** and **2-Step Verification required**. U.S. DevEx stays available until **mid-2027**, when the transition away from it begins. Other regions get a DevEx end date only once Wallet is fully live there. A **Roblox Card** follows in **2027**. Fees, minimums, group earnings and under-18 rules are **not published** (as of Oct 27, 2026).

## Confirmed (official announcement, as of Oct 27, 2026)
| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Who first | U.S.-based **independent creators (sole proprietors)**, **18+** |
| When | Rollout **starts December 2026** (gradual, not everyone on day one) |
| Payout | **USD**, paid **automatically each business day** |
| Security | **2-Step Verification required** before any payout |
| DevEx (U.S.) | Still available until **mid-2027**, then the transition begins |
| DevEx (other regions) | End date published per region **after** Wallet fully launches there |
| Buying Robux back | Wallet funds can buy Robux at the **DevEx rate ($0.0038/Robux)**, for example to fund ads or publishing ([[Developer_Exchange_DevEx]]) |
| Roblox Card | **2027**. Spend the Wallet balance wherever major cards are accepted, including Apple/Google Wallet. Not U.S.-exclusive |

The announcement reached this vault through search excerpts, because the cloud research environment can't open devforum.roblox.com directly. Read the full post before making financial decisions.

## Reported, not confirmed by Roblox
- **Airwallex as the payments partner:** press only (GamesBeat).
- **Not available in some U.S. states** (a news report names New York, Nevada, Hawaii, Indiana and Wisconsin): single secondary source.
- **UK/EU in early 2027:** a creator's RDC workshop summary on X, not an official post.
- **Earnings can be held in the Wallet for up to 30 days:** appears in a search summary of the official post but wasn't readable verbatim. Verify it.

## Still unknown
- Fees, minimum balance, payout limits and FX behavior outside the U.S.
- **Group-owned earnings.** The launch scope is sole proprietors only. Many studios earn through groups ([[Version_Control_Workflow|team setups]] often route revenue through a group).
- **Under-18 creators.** Launch eligibility is 18+. No official path is published, and DevEx itself requires 13+ (see [[Developer_Exchange_DevEx]]), and the 18+ rate depends on [[Age_Checks_and_Chat_Safety|age checks]]. If you're under 18, involve a parent or guardian and expect DevEx to stay your route for now.
- How Earned Robux balances move over when U.S. DevEx ends, and whether local-currency payouts change [[Price_Optimization_and_Regional_Pricing|regional pricing]] math.

## What to do now
1. Turn on **2-Step Verification** now. Payouts require it.
2. **Before Oct 31, 2026:** check Creator Hub ▸ **Taxes**. DevEx becomes royalty income on Nov 1, 2026, and missing or invalid tax info means 24% backup withholding ([[Developer_Exchange_DevEx]]).
3. Keep DevEx eligibility healthy: valid tax forms, verified email, no moderation strikes. DevEx stays your cash-out in the U.S. until mid-2027, and longer elsewhere.
4. If you earn through a group, don't assume Wallet covers you yet.
5. Diversify revenue so payout-timing changes don't hurt: [[Subscriptions]], [[Creator_Rewards]], [[Rewarded_Video_Ads]].
6. Track official posts ([[Release_Notes_and_Change_Tracking]], [[DevForum_Guide]]) and update this note when Creator Hub documentation lands. As of Oct 27, 2026, the Creator Docs repo has no Wallet page.
