# Roblox Wallet Transition (RDC 2026)

> **TL;DR:** At **RDC 2026 (Sept 2026)** Roblox announced a **creator Wallet** (reported partner: **Airwallex**) that pays out earnings in **local currency, as often as daily**, starting with **eligible U.S. creators around December 2026**, plus a **Roblox Card** in **2027**. One report says the U.S. will move away from classic DevEx cash-outs by **mid-2027**. ⚠️ These details come from press and creator coverage, not yet from the Creator Docs (as of Oct 27, 2026). Verify in Creator Hub ▸ Finances before planning around them.

## What's reasonably confirmed (multiple outlets)
- Wallet-based, faster payouts to creators in local currency.
- U.S. first, with broader rollout later.
- A physical/virtual card product in 2027.
- Higher payouts for 18+ verified U.S. purchases (the docs list the 18+ rate as $0.0054 vs the $0.0038 standard: [[Developer_Exchange_DevEx]]).

## What's single-source / unclear
- Exact DevEx sunset timing for U.S. creators (mid-2027 claim).
- Fees, minimums, and how existing Earned Robux balances convert.
- Eligibility for creators **under 18** (likely requires parent/guardian involvement).

## What to do now
1. Keep DevEx eligibility healthy (tax forms, verified email, compliance).
2. Make your earnings predictable: subscriptions, Creator Rewards and ads diversify income ([[Subscriptions]], [[Creator_Rewards]], [[Rewarded_Video_Ads]]).
3. Track official posts ([[Release_Notes_and_Change_Tracking]], [[DevForum_Guide]]) and update this note when the docs land.
